# E-Commerce-Sales-Performance-Analysis
An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, regional trends, payment methods, revenue drivers, and delivery performance. The project uses Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly to transform raw transaction data into meaningful business insights.
🎯 Business Problem

An e-commerce company generates a large volume of transaction data but needs a clear understanding of what is driving its revenue and where business performance can be improved.

💡 Key Insights

The analysis indicates several important relationships that can be investigated further:

Quantity and revenue show a strong positive relationship, indicating that larger order quantities are associated with higher transaction revenue.
Unit price and revenue show a positive relationship, suggesting that higher-priced products contribute to higher transaction values.
Discount and revenue show an inverse relationship in the analyzed data, which requires further investigation to understand the underlying business factors.
Delivery days do not show an obvious strong relationship with transaction revenue based on exploratory analysis.
Customer-level analysis can identify high-value customers and frequent purchasers, providing opportunities for targeted retention strategies.
